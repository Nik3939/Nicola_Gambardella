What I do:

I am pursuing a PhD in Biology combining microbial ecology and computational biology. My work focuses on microbial transformations of nitrogen and mercury in permafrost degrading environment. For my research I use metagenomics and metatranscriptomics approaches.

What I focus on:

  • Microbial community analysis
  • Mercury biogeochemistry
  • Nitrogen biogeochemistry
  • Bioinformatic pipeline and software development
